A. 3-Question Top Comment Templates for All 6 PHP Files

/* --- 1. functions.php ---
 * 1. Hierarchy role: Not a rendering template; loaded automatically by WordPress on every request before templates run.
 * 2. Why separate: Keeps theme supports, asset enqueuing, menus, and widget registration organized in one central setup file.
 * 3. Connections: Supplies styles/scripts for header.php and templates, and registers menus/widgets used by header.php and footer.php.
 */

/* --- 2. header.php ---
 * 1. Hierarchy role: Shared top-of-document and site header partial included across page templates.
 * 2. Why separate: Avoids duplicating <!doctype html>, <head>, wp_head(), social links, header widgets, and primary nav across templates.
 * 3. Connections: Called at the top of front-page.php, page.php, and index.php via get_header(); pairs with footer.php.
 */

/* --- 3. front-page.php ---
 * 1. Hierarchy role: High-priority template in the WordPress hierarchy for the static Front Page (or site front when set).
 * 2. Why separate: Lets the home page showcase a custom hero, testimonials, and 6 cards without cluttering ordinary inner pages.
 * 3. Connections: Calls get_header(), runs the WordPress Loop, loads custom template parts via get_template_part(), and calls get_footer().
 */

/* --- 4. page.php ---
 * 1. Hierarchy role: Template used for singular WordPress 'page' posts (such as inner Serbia destination pages).
 * 2. Why separate: Gives standard pages a consistent title banner, conditional featured image check, and page content layout.
 * 3. Connections: Calls get_header(), loops through the current page post with have_posts()/the_post()/the_content(), and calls get_footer().
 */

/* --- 5. index.php ---
 * 1. Hierarchy role: Ultimate fallback template at the bottom of the WordPress template hierarchy when no more specific template matches.
 * 2. Why separate: Guarantees WordPress always has a valid fallback template to render posts/archives safely.
 * 3. Connections: Calls get_header(), runs a fallback Loop with the_permalink(), the_title(), and the_excerpt(), and calls get_footer().
 */

/* --- 6. footer.php ---
 * 1. Hierarchy role: Shared bottom-of-page and closing HTML document partial included by templates.
 * 2. Why separate: Centralizes the 3 footer navigation menu columns, copyright line, and wp_footer() hook in one place.
 * 3. Connections: Called at the bottom of front-page.php, page.php, and index.php via get_footer(), closing tags opened in header.php.
 */
